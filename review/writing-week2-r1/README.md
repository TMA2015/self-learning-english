## Manual iPad Safari checklist

1. Open the preview link in **Safari**. Confirm no blank page, script error or horizontal scrolling.
2. **Learn:** Choose the museum example and type a reason. Confirm authored explanation stays hidden before your attempt and then appears.
3. **Sort ideas:** Select relevance labels; look at explanations only after answering. Use the recycling repair exercise and confirm the worked example requires an attempt.
4. **Idea board:** Tap *Load clearly labelled sample ideas*. On a narrow viewport, expand/collapse the Mind Map and confirm ideas remain editable. Add an idea card, choose it from the map, inspect the selected label, and reorder cards. Check the `best` field reflects the chosen idea.
5. **Compare:** See clearly labelled sample versions; changing the selection or writing an unsaved reflection must not claim to have saved learner work.
6. **New task:** Fill all three scratchpad fields before opening the optional process hints. No automatic grade should appear.
7. Test touch targets and text entry in portrait and landscape, with external keyboard Tab/Enter if available. Record browser/iPad version and screenshots of genuine issues.
