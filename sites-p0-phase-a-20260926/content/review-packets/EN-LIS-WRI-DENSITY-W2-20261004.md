**CEFR target:** A1

### Explanation — English

## LIS-A2-02 — A changed plan for Saturday

**Module:** listening  
**CEFR target:** A2  
**Estimated time:** 17 minutes

### Objectives

- Understand the purpose of a short conversation about a changed plan.
- Identify revised times, meeting points and things to bring.
- Make simple inferences from reasons and conditions in the conversation.

### Explanation — English

When a plan changes, listen for contrast between the old plan and the new one. Separate the meeting time from the departure time, and notice reasons such as because or so. Listen twice before answering if needed.

## LIS-B1-02 — Discussing a group-project problem

**Module:** listening  
**CEFR target:** B1  
**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

### Objectives

- Identify the main problem and proposed solution in a planning conversation.
- Understand details about deadlines, roles and constraints.
- Infer why speakers prefer one solution or explanation over another.

### Explanation — English

At B1, listen for the problem, the options speakers consider and the reason they finally prefer one option. Contrast words and practical constraints often reveal attitude and intention. Exact details such as deadlines still matter.

### Vietnamese support

## WRI-B1-02 — Build a clear opinion paragraph

**Module:** writing  
**CEFR target:** B1  
**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

### Objectives

- Analyse an opinion prompt and choose relevant ideas.
- Organise an opinion, reason, example and conclusion into a coherent paragraph.
- Use suitable linkers, register and language choices before drafting a longer response.

### Explanation — English

Before writing, identify the exact question and decide your position. Build one clear line of support: opinion → reason → specific example → concluding link. Cohesive words such as because, for example, however and therefore should show a real relationship between ideas, not simply decorate the paragraph. Clear, relevant language is more important than unnecessarily difficult vocabulary.

### Vietnamese support

Trước khi viết, hãy xác định đúng yêu cầu đề và chọn quan điểm. Xây dựng một mạch rõ ràng: ý kiến → lý do → ví dụ cụ thể → câu kết. Từ nối phải thể hiện quan hệ thật giữa các ý, không chỉ được thêm vào cho có.

### Examples

- OPINION · I think schools should give students one homework-free evening each week.
- REASON · This can help students recover and manage other responsibilities.
- EXAMPLE · For example, they could use that evening for sport, family time or reading.
- CONCLUSION · Therefore, one planned break can support a healthier weekly routine.
