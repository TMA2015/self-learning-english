### 1. AUDIT OF THE 14 LEXICAL BANK ENTRIES

All 14 multiword chunks and collocations were individually audited for accuracy, naturalness, and evidence safety:

1. **`reduce waste`** (*produce less material that is thrown away*): Model sentence (*"The cafeteria wants to reduce waste by offering portions in different sizes"*) is natural, age-appropriate, and accurate.
2. **`reusable container`** (*a box or bottle that can be used many times*): Model sentence (*"Hana brings her salad in a reusable container instead of a disposable box"*) cleanly contrasts reusable vs. single-use.
3. **`single-use packaging`** (*wrapping or containers designed to be used once*): Model sentence accurately demonstrates attribution in a school environment.
4. **`sort waste`** (*separate unwanted material by type*): Model sentence (*"Visitors sort waste into labelled paper and food bins"*) accurately reflects everyday environmental sorting.
5. **`repair rather than replace`** (*fix something instead of buying a new one*): Model sentence (*"The library chose to repair its older chairs rather than replace them"*) highlights a practical choice.
6. **`save energy`** (*use less electricity or fuel than necessary*): Model sentence (*"Switching off empty-room lights can save energy"*) provides a clear, actionable example.
7. **`public transport`** (*buses, trains and similar shared travel services open to the public*): Model sentence accurately frames shared community travel.
8. **`cut down on`** (*reduce the amount or frequency of something*): Model sentence (*"We could cut down on printing by sharing one timetable per team"*) correctly uses *cut down on + noun/-ing*.
9. **`have an impact on`** (*affect a person, place or situation*): Explicitly notes that *impact* is neutral (can be positive or negative) and takes the preposition *on*.
10. **`raise awareness of`** (*help more people learn about an issue*): Correctly highlights the verb-noun collocation (*raise*, not *rise*) and preposition *of*.
11. **`make a difference`** (*have a noticeable or useful effect*): Accurately notes that *make a difference* describes potential useful effect (*might make a difference*) without claiming absolute proof.
12. **`take practical steps`** (*do specific, realistic things rather than only discuss an issue*): Model sentence (*"The committee took practical steps such as adding a refill station"*) provides a concrete example.
13. **`conserve water`** (*use water carefully to avoid wasting it*): Model sentence (*"Rainwater collected from a roof can help conserve water during dry weeks"*) is precise and contextual.
14. **`long-term effect`** (*a result that continues or appears after a longer time*): Explicitly cautions against treating a short 1-day or 1-week observation as proof of a long-term effect.
