## VOC-A2-04 — Feelings, reactions and useful adjective pairs

**Module:** vocabulary  
**CEFR target:** A2  
**Estimated time:** 21 minutes

### Objectives

- Use common adjective–preposition combinations for feelings and reactions.
- Distinguish natural combinations such as interested in, worried about and proud of.
- Choose vocabulary from sentence meaning as well as grammar.

### Explanation — English

Many common adjectives are usually followed by a particular preposition. Useful A2 combinations include interested in, worried about, excited about, proud of, good at, afraid of, ready for and responsible for. Learn the whole phrase, not only the adjective. Check the meaning of the sentence before choosing the preposition.

### Vietnamese support

### Examples

- INTERESTED IN · I am interested in photography.
- WORRIED ABOUT · She is worried about the test.
- GOOD AT · Minh is good at drawing.
- READY FOR · We are ready for the trip.

## VOC-B1-04 — Verbs for explaining evidence and change

**Module:** vocabulary  
**CEFR target:** B1  
**Estimated time:** 24 minutes

### Objectives

- Use common B1 verbs for describing evidence, causes and changes.
- Distinguish show, suggest and prove according to strength of evidence.
- Use cause, lead to, result in, increase, decrease and remain in clear contexts.

### Explanation — English

When explaining information, choose verbs that match the strength and direction of the evidence. Show can introduce a clear pattern; suggest is more cautious; prove is much stronger and should not be used when the evidence is limited. For change, useful patterns include lead to + noun, result in + noun, cause + noun, increase/decrease, and remain + adjective or noun phrase.

### Vietnamese support

Khi mô tả bằng chứng, cần chọn động từ đúng mức độ chắc chắn. show có thể nêu một xu hướng rõ; suggest thận trọng hơn; prove rất mạnh. Với thay đổi có thể dùng lead to, result in, cause, increase, decrease, remain.

### Examples

- EVIDENCE · The survey suggests that students prefer shorter meetings.
- CAUSE · Heavy rain caused a delay.
- RESULT · Better signs led to fewer mistakes.
- STABILITY · Attendance remained stable.

## REA-A2-05 — Two club updates: compare practical details

**Module:** reading  
**CEFR target:** A2  
**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

### Objectives

- Find practical details in two short school notices.
- Compare times, places and requirements across the notices.
- Choose the best option for a simple learner need using textual evidence.

### Explanation — English

When two notices are presented together, first identify which details belong to each option. Make a small mental table: day, time, place, what to bring and whether registration is needed. For a comparison question, use only information that appears in the text.

### Vietnamese support

Khi có hai thông báo, hãy tách thông tin của từng lựa chọn: ngày, giờ, địa điểm, đồ cần mang và có cần đăng ký hay không. Câu so sánh phải dựa trên dữ kiện trong bài.

### Examples

- DETAIL · Which club is on Tuesday?
- COMPARE · Which club finishes later?
- REQUIREMENT · Which club needs registration?
- CHOICE · Which option fits a student who is free only on Tuesday?

### Reading text

**Club updates for next week**

Two clubs have changed their plans for next week. The Photography Club will now meet on Tuesday in Room 18 from 3:30 to 4:30. Students should bring a phone or camera if possible, but the club will provide paper for notes. New members are welcome and do not need to register before the meeting. The Cooking Club will meet on Wednesday in the school kitchen from 4:00 to 5:30. Students must register by Monday because the kitchen has only twelve places. They do not need to bring ingredients, but they should bring a notebook. Both clubs are free this month.
