## P01-U11 — Polite requests with can and could

**Module/target:** grammar / A1 · **Prerequisite:** P01-U02 and existing Grammar foundation (exact reusable lesson ID requires confirmation)

**CORE-1 Goal/context:** Choose can or could in clear school requests and distinguish requests from statements.

**CORE-2 Concept:** Use Can I ...? and Could I ...? to request permission politely. Could is often more tentative; neither guarantees permission. For a request, keep the base verb after the modal: Could I borrow ...? not Could I borrowing ...?

**Independent worked model:** Different model: 'Could I open the window, please?' The speaker asks permission; open is the base verb. 'I open the window' is a statement, not a permission request.

**Strategy:** 1. Identify whether the speaker is asking permission. 2. Choose can/could plus the base verb. 3. Check whether the request is polite and grammatically complete.

**Optional Vietnamese support:** Học cách làm qua ví dụ riêng, sau đó tự trả lời bài tập. Chỉ bài có đáp án khách quan mới có thể được chấm sau khi duyệt; bài viết/nói tự do không được tự chấm điểm.

**Hint:** After can/could, look for the base form of the verb.

**CORE-4 Feedback and recovery:** After Submit, contrast a grammatical permission question with a statement or an incorrect -ing form.

## P01-U13 — Prepare and practise: explaining a missed deadline

**Module/target:** speaking / A2 · **Prerequisite:** P01-U07 and P01-U12

**CORE-1 Goal/context:** Plan a respectful explanation and request for help, then practise aloud without receiving a speaking proficiency score.

**CORE-2 Concept:** A clear request has a greeting, a factual problem, one relevant reason, a specific next-step question and thanks. Do not promise an extension; ask what is possible.

**Independent worked model:** Independent model: 'Excuse me, I could not attend the club meeting because I was ill. Could you tell me what I missed? Thank you.' Notice the factual reason and specific question.

**Strategy:** 1. Write three keywords for the problem, reason and request. 2. Speak aloud using keywords instead of reading a complete script. 3. Self-check clarity and politeness; no oral performance is measured.

**Optional Vietnamese support:** Học cách làm qua ví dụ riêng, sau đó tự trả lời bài tập. Chỉ bài có đáp án khách quan mới có thể được chấm sau khi duyệt; bài viết/nói tự do không được tự chấm điểm.

**Hint:** A respectful request explains the situation without blaming someone else.

**CORE-4 Feedback and recovery:** Self-check whether the request names the problem and asks a clear question. No microphone, speech evaluation, fluency mark or CEFR result is implied.

**CORE-1 Goal/context:** Identify two changed project requirements and draft a clear reply that acknowledges them and asks one useful question.
