### C3. A1-U07 — reply to an invitation (priority gap)
Existing GRA-A1-07 teaches can/can't and WRI-A1-02 composes an invitation, not a reply. 
Goal: understand a simple invitation and respond with availability/ability, preference and one relevant detail.
Model: "Come to the music room on Friday at four. Can you sing?" Example reply: "Thanks! I can come at four. I like music, but I can't sing well." Notice can come expresses availability; can't sing expresses ability. A polite reply can accept or decline; a preference is not a reason to invent an event detail.
Fresh message: "Hi! Can you come to the games club on Tuesday at three? We can play chess or cards. Meet in Room 6. — Sam"
Fictional profile: You are free at three on Tuesday. You can play cards. You cannot play chess. You like cards. Reply in 3–4 short sentences: thank Sam, say if you can come, give your preference or ability and confirm one real detail from the message. Optional variant on another day only after completing task: practise politely declining. Self-check day/time/place, can + base verb, relevant preference, friendly tone. Weight 0; not a new Speaking score.
