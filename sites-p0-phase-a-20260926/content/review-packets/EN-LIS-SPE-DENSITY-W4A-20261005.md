## LIS-A1-02 — A classroom change for Thursday

**Module:** listening  
**CEFR target:** A1  
**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

### Objectives

- Understand the main purpose of a short school announcement.
- Identify the day, time, room and materials in the announcement.
- Distinguish the changed information from the original plan.

### Explanation — English

For a short announcement, listen first for the main purpose. On the second listen, note practical details such as day, time, room and what to bring. Words such as now, instead and changed often signal new information.

### Vietnamese support

Với một thông báo ngắn, lần nghe đầu hãy xác định mục đích chính. Lần nghe thứ hai ghi lại ngày, giờ, phòng và đồ cần mang. Các từ như now, instead, changed thường báo hiệu thông tin mới.

### Examples

- PURPOSE · Why is the teacher speaking?
- CHANGE · Which room is new?
- DETAIL · What time does the activity start?

### Listening source text

Teacher: Hello, class. Here is a quick change for Thursday's English club. We usually meet in Room 7, but another class needs that room this week. Please go to Room 14 instead. The club still starts at four o'clock and finishes at five. Bring your English notebook and a pencil. You do not need your course book because I will give you a short reading sheet. Please arrive five minutes early so we can begin on time.

## LIS-B1-03 — Planning a study-group session

**Module:** listening  
**CEFR target:** B1  
**Estimated time:** 21 minutes

### Objectives

- Identify the main problem and agreed solution in a planning conversation.
- Follow details about timing, responsibilities and materials.
- Infer why speakers accept or reject suggestions.
- Recognise polite intentions such as proposing, checking and clarifying.

### Explanation — English

In a planning conversation, speakers may suggest several options before agreeing on one. Listen for proposal language such as why don't we, could we and how about, then notice the response and final decision. Separate ideas that are only suggested from plans that are actually agreed.

### Vietnamese support

Trong hội thoại lập kế hoạch, người nói có thể đưa ra nhiều phương án trước khi chốt. Hãy chú ý các mẫu gợi ý như why don't we, could we, how about, rồi nghe phản hồi và quyết định cuối cùng. Phân biệt ý mới đề xuất với kế hoạch đã được thống nhất.

### Examples

- PROPOSAL · Why don't we meet after lunch?
- CHECK · Does 3:30 work for you?
- FINAL PLAN · So we will meet in the library at 3:30.
- REASON · I cannot stay late because I have practice.

### Listening source text

Nina: We still need to prepare for Friday's history quiz. Shall we meet after school tomorrow? Omar: I can, but I have basketball practice at five, so I need to leave by four thirty. Nina: Then how about meeting in the library at three thirty? We could spend twenty minutes reviewing dates and then practise the longer questions. Omar: That works. I made flashcards for the dates, but I have not finished the chapter summary. Nina: I can bring my summary. Could you bring the teacher's practice sheet? Omar: Yes. Should we invite Maya too? She said she wanted help with the longer answers. Nina: Good idea, but let's message her now so she has time to reply. Omar: One more thing: the library computers are often busy after school. Do we need one? Nina: Probably not. The practice sheet and our notes should be enough. Omar: Great. I'll bring the sheet and flashcards, and you bring the summary. We'll meet by the library entrance at three thirty.

## SPE-A2-02 — Explain a simple problem and ask for help

**Module:** speaking  
**CEFR target:** A2  
**Estimated time:** 16 minutes

### Objectives

- State a simple problem clearly.
- Add one useful detail or reason.
- Make a polite request for help.
- Give a short connected spoken response without reading a full script.

### Explanation — English

A useful short spoken request has three parts: say the problem, add one important detail, and ask clearly for help. Use simple linking such as because or so. Plan keywords, not a full script, then speak naturally. A text planning note is useful when no microphone is available, but it is not speaking evidence.

### Vietnamese support

Một lời nhờ giúp ngắn có ba phần: nêu vấn đề, thêm một chi tiết quan trọng và nhờ giúp rõ ràng. Dùng because hoặc so. Chỉ ghi từ khóa thay vì viết cả bài để đọc. Nếu không có micro, ghi chú kế hoạch vẫn hữu ích nhưng không được tính là bằng chứng kỹ năng nói.

### Examples

- PROBLEM · I cannot find Room 18.
- DETAIL · My club starts in ten minutes.
- REQUEST · Could you show me where it is?
- PLAN · problem → detail → polite request

## SPE-B1-02 — Compare two options and answer a follow-up

**Module:** speaking  
**CEFR target:** B1  
**Estimated time:** 19 minutes

### Objectives

- Compare two options using relevant criteria.
- State and support a clear preference.
- Acknowledge one advantage of the other option.
- Respond to a follow-up question without abandoning the main point.

### Explanation — English

For a B1 comparison, choose two or three useful criteria rather than listing every detail. Compare the options, state your preference and explain why. Acknowledge a strength of the other option if it is relevant. In a follow-up question, respond directly first, then add a reason or example.

### Vietnamese support

Khi so sánh ở B1, hãy chọn hai hoặc ba tiêu chí quan trọng thay vì liệt kê mọi chi tiết. So sánh, nêu lựa chọn và giải thích lý do. Có thể công nhận một ưu điểm của phương án còn lại. Với câu hỏi phụ, trả lời trực tiếp trước rồi thêm lý do hoặc ví dụ.

### Examples

- COMPARE · Option A is cheaper, whereas Option B gives more practice time.
- PREFERENCE · I would choose A because it fits my schedule.
- BALANCE · B has one advantage: the sessions are longer.
- FOLLOW-UP · If I had more time, I might choose B.
