## C. Source review procedure
1. Inspect each P0 Unit's *full* explanations, independent worked examples, real reading/listening sources, original practice and feedback; locate secondary links.
2. Specify an independent task matching the Unit outcome. Mark evidence as `SUPPORTED_BY_EXISTING_SOURCE`, `PARTIAL`, `GAP_CONFIRMED`, `UNASSESSED` or `UNKNOWN`; cite exact lesson/activity IDs.
3. Add a new lesson/checkpoint only for a confirmed and scoped gap; maintain old immutable activity IDs and follow independent review and actual QA gates.
4. Do not infer CEFR level attained from mapped-module counts, provisional Diagnostic B1+/B2, unassessed Writing or Speaking Preparation.
