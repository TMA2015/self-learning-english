# SLE Master Curriculum Framework v0.1 — Working Architecture (2026-11-04)
**STATUS: DESIGN DRAFT / ACADEMIC REVIEW REQUIRED / NOT DEPLOYED.**
**Normative authority:** SLE-MASTER-001 v1.1.0 on `main`. This is a descriptive curriculum design and future learning roadmap, **not** a competing SLE rulebook. Any normative academic requirement emerging from review must be reconciled through SLE-MASTER-001's revision process.
**Evidence baseline:** 1.18.1 curriculum source inventory (109 lessons/913 activities; A1 30/A2 42/B1 37/B2 0). Reviewed Writing Wave A (6/48) is an *unreleased academic PASS*. Vocabulary+Reading Wave B (6/48) remains *pending verdict*. No new material from either wave is asserted deployed.
## A. Purpose, scope and design commitments
- Design the A1–B2 foundation pathway as 40 **proposed integrated learning units** (10 per level). A unit is a curricular cluster; it **is not automatically one imported lesson**. Existing lesson IDs may support multiple units without duplicating attempts or grading.
- Pre-A1 will be a future bridge, not a precondition for every learner; C1–C2 will be future extensions. IELTS/TOEFL will be optional future applications built on the foundation, not retroactive CEFR certification.
- A1/A2 foundational transfer is first priority; B1/B2 expansion is planned with dependency checks. CEFR levels are instructional targets, never awarded proficiency credentials by mapping alone.
- Do not require all seven domains in every unit; evaluate seven domains **across the level** and distinct subskills (gist/detail/inference/purpose, listening intention, productive writing, etc.). Speaking Prepare & Practise is not oral scoring; pronunciation symbol awareness, listening discrimination and oral production need separate evidence labels.
- Every eventual *teaching lesson* must satisfy CORE-1 through CORE-4, with independent worked demonstrations, meaningful practice, misconception feedback and next action. Free deterministic core; AI optional. Listening credit requires real aligned audio and verified playback; open Writing needs trusted semantic review.
- Coverage denominators, prerequisites, level claims and difficulty are **hypotheses for independent review**. Never publish fabricated coverage percentages; map source lesson IDs and evidence to independently reviewed outcome statements.
## B. Level outcomes (draft)
| Level | Draft functional outcome | Assessment boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Handle familiar identity, routines, basic notices, simple messages and short concrete listening tasks | Independent objective comprehension and guided writing practice; no certified CEFR |
| A2 | Navigate practical problems, past/future events, comparative choices, simple email/paragraph tasks and ordinary conversations | Mixed independent comprehension and provisional writing tasks; no unvalidated oral score |
| B1 | Follow and explain connected narratives, positions, reasons and practical recommendations; plan and revise multi-paragraph writing | Support claims with evidence and show skill-specific limitations |
| B2 | Analyse and synthesise sustained arguments and source evidence, produce structured critical writing and follow extended speech | Targets only; no B2 lessons currently imported and no proficiency claim |

## D. Required cross-unit capability matrix for the next audit
| Domain | A1 target | A2 target | B1 target | B2 target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grammar | form/meaning of basic clauses, questions, time and location | tense contrasts; past/experience/future; comparison, modality | narrative aspect, conditional/relative/reported structures | flexible control of complex clauses and nuance in real contexts |
| Vocabulary | useful school/home/routine chunks; recognition + retrieval | functional transactions, events and collocations; new-context use | stance, reasons, cause-effect and topic depth | register, precision, hedging, academic multiword language |
| Reading | notices/messages: gist and explicit detail | varied practical texts: detail, simple inference and purpose | connected texts: argument, true inference, purpose and context vocabulary | longer arguments, conflicting evidence, text organisation and credibility |
| Listening | familiar short audio: gist/detail; verified MP3 | multi-turn situations, detail/intent and replay | discussion/stance/inference and changed plans | extended explanatory speech, relationships, qualification |
| Writing | short original messages/descriptions, review boundaries | prompt analysis, relevant brainstorming, email and paragraph drafting | claims/reasons/examples, organised paragraphs, revision | synthesis, counterargument and multi-paragraph argument/report |
| Speaking | safe language toolkit, prepared simple exchanges (self-practice) | practical problem/choice preparation (self-practice) | explaining ideas and solutions (self-practice) | qualified argument, summary/mediation (self-practice) |
| Pronunciation | sound awareness and clear model exposure | phoneme/stress contrasts using real audio when assessed | sentence stress/intonation awareness; unassessed production | connected-speech, prosody and comprehension support; quality gate |
**Distinguish mapped content, actual teaching and valid assessment.** A tag occurrence is not one independent evidence instance of the learning outcome; a topic name is not mastery and a self-reported speaking rehearsal is not oral proficiency.
## E. Gap-analysis procedure (no false percentages)
1. **Traceability:** assign every source lesson ID and original question ID to exactly one primary Unit, optionally secondary units; preserve original lesson version and attempt record. Verify each proposed Unit's learning outcomes have actual learner-facing concept teaching and fresh independent items.
2. **Coverage status per Unit/outcome:** `NOT_MAPPED`, `DRAFT_TARGET`, `CONTENT_PRESENT`, `PARTIAL_TEACHING`, `ACADEMIC_REVIEWED`, `QA_VERIFIED`, `LIVE_VERIFIED`; keep assessment quality separate from publication. Do not collapse these into an invented completion %.
3. **Subskill dimension:** tag and manually inspect objective/genre/cognitive demand and sound/media evidence. Count distinct independent passages/scripts/task forms rather than repeatedly tagging the same 10 MCQs.
4. **Dependency audit:** verify DAG, no circular or broken prerequisites; check A1/A2 building blocks before B1/B2. Advisory prereqs must not be represented as application-enforced unlocks.
5. **Retire overlap deliberately:** consolidate near-duplicate targets at Unit level while retaining source lesson IDs/versions and historic results. Use new forward-only versions for any scored meaning/answer change.
## F. Proposed rollout after independent academic review
**Wave 0: audit & design** — independently review this 40-unit blueprint, reconcile required changes, map existing 109 lessons, record real gaps and save Framework v1.0. **No content import here.**
**Wave 1: A1–A2 prerequisite repair** — improve true conceptual teaching and gap units, balanced receptive+productive practice with safeguards; preferentially re-use reviewed lessons.
**Wave 2: B1 completeness & transfer** — support sustained arguments, multi-source reading and authentic listening plus writing process; do not infer mastery from easy recognition.
**Wave 3: B2 build-out** — start with real evidence/critical reading/vocabulary, then audio and extended Writing; phase audio/media production and review separately.
**Wave 4: enrichment and optional exam prep** — map exam strategies to already validated competences, without replacing mainstream learner pathway.
## G. Open academic decisions for NotebookLM / Claude
1. Are ten integrated Units per CEFR level defensible, or should units split/merge to match CEFR can-do descriptions and age profile? Treat count as *provisional*, not a quota.
2. Are any critical A1/A2 foundations missing, and are A2/B1 transitions and B2 targets too abrupt? Specify exact Units and corrective learning outcomes.
3. Is the seven-domain capability matrix balanced, and does it distinguish reception, production, interaction and mediation without overclaiming oral assessment?
4. Are cognitive demands and text/audio lengths suitable for independently learning school-age students? Avoid over-advanced B2 requirements at A2/B1.
5. Which units lack an explicit real-world communicative purpose, and which overlap excessively or have circular dependencies?
6. Can the documented 109-lesson inventory support a defensible mapping? Require source-specific evidence and mark unknowns rather than claiming coverage.
7. Should Pre-A1 bridge and C1/C2 expansion be designed in more detail now, or kept outside v1.0? Give a reason.
8. Separate blocking corrections from optional improvements; propose precise replacement Unit wording and a sequential, feasible development roadmap.
