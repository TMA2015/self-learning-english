# G Learning — Gemini Context v1.0, 2026-09-28
Project: private existing Self-Learning English, free-first English self-study for Grade 6 onward. Seven domains: Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking, Pronunciation; foundational A1/A2 with selective B1, later IELTS Reading/Writing and genuine media-based Listening/Speaking. Original content only.

Layers: immutable Content Library ≠ Course Template ≠ authenticated My Courses / explicit active selection ≠ global Evidence. A question may contribute to several courses but is one global event. Your Journey shows ONLY explicitly active course; browsing Catalog does not select. Week 1 of Writing 12 Weeks has four published interactive process stages; Weeks 2–12 are Preview. A Writing submission/revision/rubric score are separate, and pending review gives no automatic proficiency score. Text-only activity cannot establish real listening or speaking level. Target CEFR is not learner certification.
