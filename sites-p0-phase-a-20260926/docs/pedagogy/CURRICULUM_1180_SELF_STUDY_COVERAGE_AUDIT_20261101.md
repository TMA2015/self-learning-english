2. Listening: 18 existing lessons; protect real-audio playback gate and hidden transcript, add context/gist/detail/inference teaching stages.
3. Vocabulary: 14 lessons; add recall from memory, chunks and transfer without confusing free-form use with machine-scored proficiency.
4. Speaking: 6 lessons; rename/teach as Prepare & Practise and distinguish language-knowledge checks from actual oral evidence. New planning/self-report oral weight 0.
5. Pronunciation: 5 lessons; distinguish spelling/IPA awareness from hearing discrimination; any new auditory score requires verified real audio.
6. Grammar: 40 lessons; keep approved structured teaching design, review weak explanations/examples and transfer coverage without reshaping good lessons unnecessarily.
7. Writing: 11 Foundation lessons plus separate existing course; strengthen guided task analysis and feedback calibration, maintain optional AI and data safeguards.
