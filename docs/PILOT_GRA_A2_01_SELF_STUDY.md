# Pilot lesson — GRA-A2-01 (content/editorial draft, NOT deployed)

**Title:** Present simple or present continuous?  
**Audience:** early secondary learner; A2 target (not a certified CEFR level).  
**Content:** independently authored; do not copy textbook exercises.  
**Compatibility:** keep original question IDs/attempt history. Existing bounded importer accepts at most ten activities in one lesson, so expanding the present six to ten is the content-only upper bound until schema changes are tested.

## 1. What you will learn

After this lesson, distinguish a habit from an action happening now or a temporary situation; choose present simple or present continuous from meaning, not only time words; form positive, negative and question sentences; know that state verbs such as `know` are usually not used in the continuous here.

**Prerequisite check (unscored):** Can you write `he plays` and `they are playing`? If not, review pronoun + be and third-person `-s/-es`.

## 2. Learn: meaning first

| Meaning | Present simple | Present continuous |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Habit / routine | Maya walks to school on most days. | — |
| General fact | Water freezes at 0°C under standard pressure. | — |
| Happening now | — | Look! Maya is walking to the gate. |
| Temporary arrangement | — | This week, Maya is staying with her aunt. |

**Forms:** `I/you/we/they play`; `he/she/it plays`. Negative: `I don't play`; `she doesn't play` (not `doesn't plays`). Question: `Does she play?`. Continuous: `am/is/are + verb-ing`, e.g. `She isn't playing`; `Are they playing?`.

**Decision prompt:** “Is this the person's normal routine, or something happening now/temporarily?” Words like *usually*, *now*, and *this week* are clues; meaning still decides the tense.

**Vietnamese support:** Hiện tại đơn: thói quen/sự thật chung. Hiện tại tiếp diễn: đang diễn ra hoặc có tính tạm thời. Chú ý `he/she/it + -s`, và `am/is/are + V-ing`. Khi đề có dấu `___`, chỉ điền **từ/cụm từ còn thiếu**, không viết lại chủ ngữ đã có.

## 3. Worked examples — show the reasoning

**A.** Every Saturday, Lan ___ at the community centre.  
- “Every Saturday” = routine; Lan = singular third person.
- Answer: `volunteers`. Not `volunteer` (missing -s), not `is volunteering` for the normal routine intended here.

**B.** Listen! The children ___ in the next room.  
- This is happening now; “children” = plural.
- Answer: `are singing`. Not `is singing` (wrong agreement).

**C.** She ___ the answer.  
- `know` describes a state in this context.
- Answer: `knows`. Not `is knowing`.

## 6. Transfer and self-check (unscored until supported)

Write **two original sentences** about your own week: one habit and one temporary activity. Underline your verb phrase. Checklist: Does the meaning match the tense? Did you use third-person `-s` when necessary? Does the continuous include `am/is/are`? This task must not silently be graded as proficiency evidence.

## 7. Mistake routing

Preserve a broad mastery skill (`tense-and-aspect`) **and** a fine-grained tag:
- `present-simple/routine`
- `present-continuous/now-or-temporary`
- `subject-verb-agreement`
- `auxiliary-form-or-order`
- `stative-verb`
- `answer-format`

After an error, show: (1) what meaning or form the learner missed, (2) one correct contrasting example, (3) a new analogous item that is not the identical question. A correct retry is additional evidence, not retroactive deletion of the first error.
